# H2O Hackathon Context

Hydra was built for the H2O Hackathon water-supply challenge: turn California water data into something communities can understand and act on.

The 2026 event was the Ninth Annual H2O Hackathon, hosted at the San Joaquin County Office of Education on May 2, 2026. SJCOE described the event as a community-supported competition where students use coding, multimedia, and problem-solving skills to address California water issues. After the event, SJCOE reported that approximately 200 high school and college students on 58 teams competed.

Hydra's product direction maps directly to that brief:

- Make the water situation understandable to non-experts.
- Combine snowpack, precipitation, and reservoir storage instead of over-relying on one number.
- Highlight patterns that require action, such as weak future supply despite adequate current reservoirs.
- Translate the data into different planning implications for citizens, farmers, and supply managers.
